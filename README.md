A make.fun entry for the "Fortune Cookie for the Liquidated Trader" bounty.
It writes one fortune cookie line for a crypto trader who has just been
liquidated — the kind of line you crack open at 3am with an empty wallet
and a stubborn sense of humour still intact.

## The fortune

> Your horoscope says today is your lucky day; your liquidation email respectfully disagrees.

Two oracles, one trader: the horoscope promises a lucky day, and the
liquidation email politely declines to agree. Rueful, warm, and over in a
single breath — the joke lands on the loss, not on the trader.

## Why this line

A good fortune for a liquidated trader needs both halves of the brief:
the small, oracular cookie voice and the loss itself. The horoscope
supplies the voice; the liquidation email supplies the loss. Neither
side lectures the trader, and the punchline is a disagreement between
two pieces of mail, not a pun about leverage, dips, or the moon.

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the fortune to `/output/entry.txt` (no trailing newline) and
nothing else. The line is 13 words and 91 characters, inside the gate's
1–160 characters and at most 20 words.
